## Cause of the layered-NLL difference (REQUEST_FIGURES_v2 B-3), T=0.0005, NLL per site

| check | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| chat values | -1.2140 | -1.6950 | -1.9490 |
| (a) repository, seeds 1–6, every 4th snapshot (chat sample size) | -1.2138 | -1.6949 | -1.9490 |
| all 40 seeds (plotted) | -0.9336 | -1.3738 | -1.5788 |
| seeds 7–40 | -0.8885 | -1.3245 | -1.5210 |
| (c) seeds 1–6, Fitter L=8 | -1.2321 | -1.7299 | -1.9757 |
| (c) all 40 seeds, Fitter L=8 | -0.9646 | -1.4362 | -1.6334 |
| (c) all 40 seeds, L=6, product Gauss–Legendre grid 180×360 instead of Lebedev 131 | -0.9334 | -1.3738 | -1.5788 |

(a) The chat values are reproduced to 4 digits by seeds 1–6, so the difference is a pure sample effect, not a data-generation or code difference.  (b) Per-seed pooled A (25 snapshots each): seeds 1–6 mean -1.226 (range -1.334…-1.021), seeds 7–40 mean -0.962 (std 0.47, range -1.54…0.74); seeds 1–6 are defect-free (3×3 defects per snapshot 0.00 vs 0.31 for the rest), more ordered (mean |ψ_cell| 0.973 vs 0.942) and have a smaller out-of-plane ⟨ε²⟩ (0.087 vs 0.115); per-seed A correlates with ⟨ε²⟩ (r=0.99), |ψ_cell| (r=-0.96) and defect number (r=0.83).  A random 6-seed subset is as ordered as seeds 1–6 with probability ≈5% (20000 draws).  (c) Raising the multipole order from 6 to 8 lowers all layers by 0.02–0.06 nats/site (the family L=6 is slightly too small for these sharp distributions, so the layered values are upper bounds on the NLL of a richer family, i.e. lower bounds on information), the quadrature has no effect (<2×10⁻⁴).  (d) (E+J)/T at T=0.0005 is 0.9260 (first 20 snapshots of 20 seeds) vs 0.9269 (last 20), i.e. stationary; over the four temperatures (E+J)/T = 0.9164 + 0.397 √T (rms residual 0.001), whose intercept equals 11/12 = 0.9167; I could not compare with the chat data directly, but the chat's 'about 0.92' is consistent with 0.925.

8 of 38 compared quantities differ by more than 10%.  Notes: chat values came from small samples (about 6 seeds / 100 snapshots).  The kagome layered NLLs (A, B, C) differ by 0.28–0.37 nats/site because the chat sample was seeds 1–6, which are defect-free and more ordered (see the B-3 section); with seeds 1–6 the chat values are reproduced exactly.  The (iv) and VAE (ii) values agree exactly.
